# Learning Links Foundation — Internship Experience

I was selected for an internship opportunity with the **Learning Links Foundation (LLF)**, where I had the opportunity to mentor and engage with government-school students through interactive and practical learning experiences.

During my internship, I:

* Mentored government-school students across various **domain fields**, helping them explore concepts through practical and engaging learning methods.
* Guided students in **creating real-life projects**, encouraging creativity, problem-solving, and application of knowledge.
* Designed and conducted **gamification-based activities** to make learning more interactive, engaging, and enjoyable.
* Encouraged students to connect classroom concepts with **real-world applications** through hands-on activities and project-based learning.
* Supported students in developing **communication, teamwork, creativity, and critical-thinking skills**.

This experience strengthened my skills in **mentoring, communication, leadership, project coordination, and student engagement**, while allowing me to contribute to meaningful educational initiatives for government-school students.
